# DevFest Tulsa

DevFest Tulsa is a larger Google Developer Group event that [GDG Tulsa](../orgs/gdg-tulsa.md) is planning. It was announced as coming up at the [November 19, 2026 meetup](google-dev-x-women-in-tech-2026-11-19.md).

- No date or registration page had been published as of November 20, 2026.
- Where it will appear: the [GDG Tulsa chapter page](https://gdg.community.dev/gdg-tulsa/). Join the chapter there to hear about it.
- The chapter page describes laying groundwork for DevFest-style events as part of its 2026 focus.
