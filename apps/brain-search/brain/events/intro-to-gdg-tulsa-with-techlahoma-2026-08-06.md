# Intro to GDG Tulsa with Techlahoma (Aug 6, 2026)

The first [GDG Tulsa](../orgs/gdg-tulsa.md) session, a beginner workshop held with [Techlahoma](../orgs/techlahoma.md).

- Date: Thursday, August 6, 2026, 6:00 PM to 8:00 PM (CDT).
- Venue: "Gradient: Tulsa's Hub for Innovation," 12 North Cheyenne Avenue, Tulsa, OK 74103 (see [Gradient](../orgs/gradient.md)).
- Described as "a light, hands-on workshop made for beginners"; "No prior experience is required."
- Host: GDG Tulsa. Techlahoma is listed as "Partner."
- Speakers: [Zain Khan](../people/zain-khan.md), "GDG Tulsa lead organizer," and a co-host described as a Techlahoma Board Member.
- A matching Luma listing, "Intro to Google Developer Group," was presented by the Techlahoma Calendar with Techlahoma Foundation among the hosts.
