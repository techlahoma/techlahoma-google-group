# ThunderPlains

ThunderPlains is a web and mobile developer conference in Oklahoma City organized by [Techlahoma](../orgs/techlahoma.md).

- Techlahoma describes it as "a web and mobile developer conference organized by Techlahoma," focused on JavaScript and related web technologies.
- ThunderPlains 2026 was a one-day conference on October 21, 2026, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM, at the OKC Convention Center.
- Tickets were sold through Techlahoma's ti.to page.
- Its Tulsa counterpart is [200OK](200ok.md).
