# 200OK

200OK is [Techlahoma](../orgs/techlahoma.md)'s one-day developer conference in Tulsa.

- Techlahoma's conferences page invites people to "Connect with fellow web professionals" at a one-day event with talks on current web technologies, hands-on hacking sessions, breakouts and group discussions.
- Its 2027 early-list page calls it "Techlahoma's Tulsa developer conference" and says that in 2026 "200OK brought Oklahoma builders together for a full day of practitioner talks, demos" and community connections.
- As of 2026-11-18 the 2027 date is not announced: "Tickets, date, venue, capacity, and pricing will be announced later."
- The Tulsa Techlahoma Meetup lists a past 200OK event on May 23, 2025.
- Its Oklahoma City counterpart is [ThunderPlains](thunderplains.md).
