# Tulsa Tech Week

Tulsa Tech Week is a week of concurrent tech events across Tulsa, Oklahoma.

- Its site says: "Tulsa Tech Week is where innovation meets opportunity!"
- The 2026 edition ran September 21-26, 2026, in Tulsa.
- The FAQ describes it as "an initiative with the support of public and private partnerships."
- Stated mission: to "promote technology to the people of Tulsa" and give residents chances to engage with technology in their communities.
- The site says "numerous events" run at the same time; it gives no total count.
