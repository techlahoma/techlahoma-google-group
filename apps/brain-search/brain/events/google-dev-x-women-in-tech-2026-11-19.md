# Google Dev × Women+ in Tech: Actual Productivity with AI

Thursday, November 19, 2026, 6–8 PM CST, at [GitWit](../orgs/gitwit.md), 301 E Archer St, Tulsa.

[GDG Tulsa](../orgs/gdg-tulsa.md) and [Women+ in Technology of Tulsa](../ecosystem/women-plus-in-tech-tulsa.md) teamed up with [Techlahoma](../orgs/techlahoma.md) for an evening about getting real work off your plate with AI. The event page lists these live demos:

- Customer support chats: let an AI agent sit through the "what's your account PIN, what's your email, let me escalate that" loop.
- Clearing out Friday: clear tomorrow's calendar and reschedule every meeting in one go.
- Finding things in a pile of messy notes.

No engineering background was needed, and attending required no install or purchase. Registration was free with host approval.
