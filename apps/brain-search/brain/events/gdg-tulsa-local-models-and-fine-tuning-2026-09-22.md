# GDG Tulsa: Local Models & Fine-Tuning (Sep 22, 2026)

A [GDG Tulsa](../orgs/gdg-tulsa.md) meetup with [OklahomAI Developers Tulsa](../orgs/oklahomai-developers-tulsa.md) and [Techlahoma](../orgs/techlahoma.md) on running and adapting small open AI models, held during [Tulsa Tech Week](tulsa-tech-week.md).

- Date: Tuesday, September 22, 2026, 6:00 to 8:00 PM (CDT).
- Venue: [Gitwit](../orgs/gitwit.md), 301 East Archer Street, Tulsa, OK 74120.
- Topic, from the event page: cloud APIs are not the only way to build with AI. Smaller open models can run on a laptop, stay close to your data, and be adapted for a specific job.
- Four demos you can still run in a browser at [gdg-model-workshop.web.app](https://gdg-model-workshop.web.app/): [context](https://gdg-model-workshop.web.app/#context), [retrieval](https://gdg-model-workshop.web.app/#retrieval), [fine-tuning](https://gdg-model-workshop.web.app/#fine-tuning), and [tiny-model training](https://gdg-model-workshop.web.app/#train).
- What to bring, per the kit: a laptop with a current version of Chrome. Models download on first use, roughly 300 to 470 MB each.
- Kit: the [GDG Tulsa Model Workshop Kit](https://samcarltoncreative.notion.site/GDG-Tulsa-Model-Workshop-Kit-3dc012b9b020817e93b7dac0365ca614) has the demos and what you need to keep experimenting. App source is in the [repository](https://github.com/techlahoma/techlahoma-google-group/tree/main/apps/building-ai-models-without-coding).
- More: [all GDG Tulsa meetups so far](../notes/gdg-tulsa-meetups-and-crossovers.md).
