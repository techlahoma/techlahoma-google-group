# GDG Tulsa workshop kits and demos

Where to find the materials from each [GDG Tulsa](../orgs/gdg-tulsa.md) meetup: the kit, the live demos, and the code. Everything is free to use.

## November 19, 2026: Actual Productivity with AI

- [Workshop page](https://sam.lc/wit): every demo, prompt, and link from the night.
- [wit-demos.web.app](https://wit-demos.web.app/): the demos with prompts to copy. [Managing email](https://wit-demos.web.app/mail/), [recapping meetings](https://wit-demos.web.app/meeting-followups/), [agent memories](https://wit-demos.web.app/agent-memory/), [a dashboard you can check](https://wit-demos.web.app/dashboard/), and [a personal search page](https://wit-demos.web.app/personal-google/).
- [The checkable-dashboard skill](checkable-dashboard-skill.md): an agent skill for building dashboards whose numbers you can check.
- Event: [Google Dev × Women+ in Tech](../events/google-dev-x-women-in-tech-2026-11-19.md).

## October 29, 2026: Put Agent Skills to Work

- Event: [GDG WordPress: Put Agent Skills to Work](../events/gdg-wordpress-put-agent-skills-to-work-2026-10-29.md). Related idea: [agent skills](agent-skills.md).

## September 22, 2026: Local Models & Fine-Tuning

- [GDG Tulsa Model Workshop Kit](https://samcarltoncreative.notion.site/GDG-Tulsa-Model-Workshop-Kit-3dc012b9b020817e93b7dac0365ca614).
- [The four demos](https://gdg-model-workshop.web.app/): context, retrieval, fine-tuning, and tiny-model training, in your browser.
- Event: [Local Models & Fine-Tuning](../events/gdg-tulsa-local-models-and-fine-tuning-2026-09-22.md).

## August 6, 2026: Intro to Google Developer Group

- [GDG Tulsa Builder Kit](https://app.notion.com/p/samcarltoncreative/GDG-Tulsa-Builder-Kit-3b2012b9b02081eebdc7dea8c5cbd69f).
- [Tulsa Gravity Rally](https://tulsa-gravity-rally.web.app/host): a racing game built for the night that you join from your phone.
- Event: [Intro to GDG Tulsa with Techlahoma](../events/intro-to-gdg-tulsa-with-techlahoma-2026-08-06.md).
