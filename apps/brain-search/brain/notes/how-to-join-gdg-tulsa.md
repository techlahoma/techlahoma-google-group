# How to join GDG Tulsa

[GDG Tulsa](../orgs/gdg-tulsa.md) is free and open to all backgrounds and experience levels. You do not need to be an engineer.

- Join the chapter: [gdg.community.dev/gdg-tulsa](https://gdg.community.dev/gdg-tulsa/). New events and sign-ups are posted there.
- Find the next meetup: follow [Techlahoma Events on Luma](https://luma.com/calendar/cal-CNuOfIVzrRIe4AC), where meetups are listed with registration.
- Ask questions between meetups: [join Techlahoma Slack](https://www.techlahoma.org/techlahoma-slack/) and open the `#ug-google` channel. See [Techlahoma Slack](../ecosystem/techlahoma-slack.md).
- What a meetup is like: hands-on and practical. Follow along on a laptop or a phone, pair with someone, or just watch.
- Conduct: the [Google Developer Group Event Code of Conduct](https://support.google.com/developergroups/answer/3340512) and the [Techlahoma Code of Conduct](https://www.techlahoma.org/code-of-conduct/) both apply.
- Past meetups: [meetups and crossovers](gdg-tulsa-meetups-and-crossovers.md). Materials: [kits and demos](gdg-tulsa-workshop-kits-and-demos.md).
- GDG Tulsa is volunteer-run and independently organized. It is not endorsed by Google.
