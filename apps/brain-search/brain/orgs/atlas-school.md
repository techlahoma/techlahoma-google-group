# Atlas School

Atlas School is a Tulsa coding school that trains people for software careers.

- Tagline: "Your path to a career in tech®." The site says it teaches "in-demand coding skills for a career in software development" and that "No previous coding experience required."
- Location: 15 N Cheyenne Ave, Tulsa, OK 74103.
- Programs listed (checked 2026-11-18): Full-Stack Development, Machine Learning, Systems Programming, and Corporate Classes.

## Sources

- [Atlas School](https://www.atlasschool.com/)

## On the Tulsa Ecosystem Map

A software development school in downtown Tulsa, formerly Holberton School Tulsa. It teaches full-stack development, machine learning and systems programming. The school says it opened in 2020 with Holberton School and the George Kaiser Family Foundation, that the foundation became its sole owner in 2022, and that it took the Atlas School name in 2024. Discussed in the Techlahoma Slack as offering employers six-week student capstone projects, such as websites, apps and databases, in 2025.
