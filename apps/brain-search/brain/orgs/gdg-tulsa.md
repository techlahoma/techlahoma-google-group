# GDG Tulsa

GDG Tulsa is a newly launched Google Developer Group for the Tulsa developer community.

- Part of the [Google Developer Groups](google-developer-groups.md) program. The chapter page says it is independent and not affiliated with or endorsed by Google.
- 2026 focus, per its page: intro and intermediate sessions on AI, Gemini and multimodal workflows; Google Cloud, AI Studio and automation tools; hands-on workshops and build nights; local community talks; partnerships with universities, bootcamps, nonprofits and local tech organizations; groundwork for DevFest-style events.
- Open to all backgrounds and experience levels. The page listed 58 members (checked 2026-11-18).
- Organizer listed: [Zain Khan](../people/zain-khan.md), GDG Organizer.
- Past events listed: [Intro to GDG Tulsa with Techlahoma](../events/intro-to-gdg-tulsa-with-techlahoma-2026-08-06.md) (Aug 6, 2026), "Expert AMA: Data Before AI" (Aug 25, 2026), "GDG TULSA: Local Models & Fine-Tuning" (Sep 22, 2026) and [GDG WordPress: Put Agent Skills to Work](../events/gdg-wordpress-put-agent-skills-to-work-2026-10-29.md) (Oct 29, 2026).
- Partners on its events include [Techlahoma](techlahoma.md); [Gitwit](gitwit.md) sponsored and hosted the Oct 29, 2026 event.
- As of 2026-11-18 the chapter page showed no upcoming events.
- All meetups and the groups they were paired with: [meetups and crossovers](../notes/gdg-tulsa-meetups-and-crossovers.md). Materials: [workshop kits and demos](../notes/gdg-tulsa-workshop-kits-and-demos.md). Taking part: [how to join](../notes/how-to-join-gdg-tulsa.md).

## On the Tulsa Ecosystem Map

Google Developer Group Tulsa, a local chapter in Google's community program for developers. It launched in 2026 and has co-hosted events with Techlahoma. It is a partner of Techlahoma, not one of its user groups. Its chapter page lists Gitwit as its local sponsor. Discussed in the Techlahoma Slack as holding a joint local-models and fine-tuning meetup with OklahomAI Developers Tulsa at Gitwit, and a free beginner-friendly introduction with Techlahoma in 2026. It also co-hosted an agent-skills event with Tulsa WordPress Meetup in October 2026.
