# Gradient (formerly 36 Degrees North)

Gradient is Tulsa's innovation and entrepreneurship hub, formerly known as 36 Degrees North.

- The Tulsa Regional Chamber called it "Tulsa's new $40 million innovation hub" at 12 N. Cheyenne Ave., with more than 110,000 square feet of workspace and programming.
- Its grand opening was on April 10, 2025 (the Chamber article was published Friday, April 11, 2025, and says the opening was Thursday).
- The article identifies Justin McLaughlin of the Tulsa Regional Chamber as chair of Gradient's board.
- GDG Tulsa's event page names the venue "Gradient: Tulsa's Hub for Innovation," 12 North Cheyenne Avenue, Tulsa, OK 74103. [GDG Tulsa](gdg-tulsa.md) held its first event there on Aug 6, 2026.
- Older Meetup pages still use the 36°N name (for example [Tulsa Web Devs](tulsa-web-devs.md) lists 36°N as its meeting space).

## On the Tulsa Ecosystem Map

A nonprofit entrepreneur hub and coworking space in downtown Tulsa, formerly called 36 Degrees North. It offers coworking memberships, private offices, meeting rooms, event space, mentorship and programs for people starting a business, and it hosts startup events such as Global Entrepreneurship Week. The name changed in 2024. Recommended in the Techlahoma Slack as a first step for meeting Tulsa entrepreneurs, through events held when the space was still called 36 Degrees North. Black Dog Law has its office here, and the firm's founder serves on Gradient's board.
