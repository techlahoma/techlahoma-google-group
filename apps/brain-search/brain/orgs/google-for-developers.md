# Google for Developers

Google for Developers (developers.google.com) is Google's hub for developer products, docs, events and communities.

- Product areas linked from the home page include Android, Chrome, Google Play, Google AI Studio, the Gemini API, Gemini CLI, Gemini Code Assist, Firebase, Flutter, Google Antigravity, Google Cloud and Colab (checked 2026-11-18).
- The home page also links accelerator programs, online and in-person developer events, developer communities and AI skills learning.
- Community programs include [Google Developer Groups](google-developer-groups.md), GDG on Campus and Google Developer Experts.
- Related notes: [Google AI Studio](../products/google-ai-studio.md), [Gemini API](../products/gemini-api.md), [Google Antigravity](../products/google-antigravity.md), [Gemini CLI](../products/gemini-cli.md).
