# Gitwit

Gitwit is a Tulsa venture studio that also hosts local tech community events.

- Gitwit's site describes it as "A venture studio driven by deep human research," located in Tulsa, Oklahoma, on East Archer Street.
- Per its site, it works inside real organizations to find problems, tests which opportunities are worth pursuing, and builds products with researchers, strategists, designers and engineers. Full-time co-founders then take over the companies it launches, with Gitwit staying on as a partner.
- [GDG Tulsa](gdg-tulsa.md)'s "GDG WordPress: Put Agent Skills to Work" event on Oct 29, 2026 was held at Gitwit, 301 East Archer Street, Tulsa, with Gitwit listed as sponsor.
- The Tulsa Techlahoma Meetup lists a past "Tulsa Techlahoma Night" (Feb 19) at "GitWit Studios".
- Identity check: these sources tie the name to the same East Archer Street, Tulsa location. An unrelated open-source project also named Gitwit exists on GitHub; it is not this org.
