# Google Developer Groups

Google Developer Groups (GDG) is Google's program of local developer community groups.

- Google's page describes it as a way to "Connect, learn, and grow alongside Google's technologies and experts."
- The page lists "2000+" community groups (checked 2026-11-18).
- Related programs on the same page: GDG on Campus (for students building tech-career skills) and Google Developer Experts (recognized experts in Google technologies).
- Chapters have pages on gdg.community.dev. Chapter pages such as [GDG Tulsa](gdg-tulsa.md)'s state that the chapter is independent and not affiliated with or endorsed by Google.
- The program sits under [Google for Developers](google-for-developers.md).
