# Tulsa Remote

Tulsa Remote is a program that pays remote workers to move to Tulsa, Oklahoma.

- Its site says: "We offer $10,000 grant to help you make Tulsa your new home base and your new home." (checked 2026-11-18)
- It invites remote workers to "make Tulsa your new headquarters and home," with financial incentives and community support.
- The site says: "We've welcomed over 4,000 talented remote workers to our Tulsa community since the program's launch in late 2018."
- Eligibility details are on a separate "Check Eligibility" page that was not read for this note.

## On the Tulsa Ecosystem Map

A program that gives a relocation grant to remote workers who move to Tulsa and connects them through a member community. It is for people with full-time remote jobs at companies outside Oklahoma. Its site says funding comes primarily from the George Kaiser Family Foundation.
