# Oklahoma Women in Technology (OKWIT)

OKWIT is an Oklahoma nonprofit of technology professionals that works to close the gender gap in STEM.

- OKWIT describes itself as a 501(c)(3) nonprofit and says: "We promote, advocate, and provide thought leadership for women working in technology."
- It is volunteer-run and funded through corporate sponsorships, memberships and donations.
- Programs listed: STEM scholarships of $500 to $2,500, two free student retreats a year for high school women (including a Tulsa Student Retreat and an OKC Student Retreat), a Women in Tech Summit held with InnoTech OK, monthly professional development events, the Trailblazer Award and an annual Gala.
- [Techlahoma](techlahoma.md)'s home page lists OKWIT as a sponsor or partner. Techlahoma's own user groups page shows no separate "Women in Tech" group (checked 2026-11-18).
- See also [SheCodes Tulsa](shecodes-tulsa.md), a Tulsa women-in-tech Meetup group.

## On the Tulsa Ecosystem Map

A nonprofit for women who work in technology in Oklahoma, started in 2016. It runs networking and professional development events, mentor circles, student retreats in Tulsa and Oklahoma City, scholarships and an annual gala. Techlahoma shows it among its sponsors and partners, and it was a listed partner of Tulsa Tech Week and of the OKC Tech Week launch party. Discussed in the Techlahoma Slack as holding monthly professional development events in Oklahoma City.
