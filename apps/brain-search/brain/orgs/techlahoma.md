# Techlahoma

Techlahoma is an Oklahoma tech community nonprofit that runs user groups, workshops, networking fairs and conferences.

- The site calls Techlahoma "Oklahoma's Largest Technology Network" with over 7,000 members, and says "The Techlahoma Foundation is a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization." (checked 2026-11-18)
- Mission, per the site: "We enrich Oklahoma's technologists of all backgrounds through education, connection, and opportunity."
- The site reports over 18 Meetup groups established and over 2,359 events hosted.
- Tulsa user groups listed on the user groups page include AWS Tulsa, Tulsa Agile Practitioners, Tulsa Game Devs, Tulsa Tech Entrepreneurs, Tulsa UX, [Tulsa Web Devs](tulsa-web-devs.md) and Tulsa.Net. Oklahoma City groups include OKC Coffee and Code, OKC Web Devs and SheCodesOKC.
- No group named "Women in Tech" and no GDG appear on the user groups page as of 2026-11-18. [Oklahoma Women in Technology (OKWIT)](okwit.md) appears on the home page as a sponsor or partner.
- Conferences: [ThunderPlains](../events/thunderplains.md) in Oklahoma City and [200OK](../events/200ok.md) in Tulsa.
- Programs linked from the site include OK Coders, Google Career Certificates, an ISC2 cybersecurity certificate, a networking fair and a Techlahoma Slack.
- The "Tulsa Techlahoma" Meetup group lists 2,243 members (checked 2026-11-18).
- Techlahoma was a partner on [GDG Tulsa](gdg-tulsa.md)'s first event, [Intro to GDG Tulsa with Techlahoma](../events/intro-to-gdg-tulsa-with-techlahoma-2026-08-06.md).

## On the Tulsa Ecosystem Map

The largest tech community in Oklahoma, with 7,000 members in its Slack. It is a statewide nonprofit, formally the Techlahoma Foundation, a 501(c)(3). Launched in 2014, it supports user groups, runs conferences and training, and hosts a Slack workspace for technologists across the state. Its mission is to enrich Oklahoma's technologists of all backgrounds through education, connection and opportunity. Techlahoma is the official tech partner of Google Developer Group Tulsa, a community group in Google's developer program.
