# SheCodes Tulsa

SheCodes Tulsa is a Tulsa Meetup group for women in tech (its Meetup address is wwc-tulsa).

- Its Meetup page says: "SheCodes Tulsa is a meetup for women in tech to come together." It planned two meetups a month: a study night and a topic-based session.
- The page lists 548 members (checked 2026-11-18).
- The page links Techlahoma's code of conduct, and one past event was titled "Techlahoma Hack Night at 36°N with Child Care". It does not state a formal affiliation with [Techlahoma](techlahoma.md).
- As of 2026-11-18 no upcoming events are listed; the most recent past event shown is "Friday Coffee Meet & Greet" on May 31, 2024.
- Techlahoma's user groups page lists a sister group in Oklahoma City, SheCodesOKC.
- See also [Oklahoma Women in Technology (OKWIT)](okwit.md).
