# Google Data Analytics Professional Certificate

An online Google Career Certificate in data analytics, offered on Coursera. It needs no prior experience and teaches spreadsheets, SQL, Tableau, Python and how to present findings from data.

Also called: Google Data Analytics Certificate, Google data analytics cert, Google Data Analytics Career Certificate.

**Example:** A founder's first operations hire takes the Google Data Analytics Certificate to learn to build sales reports in spreadsheets and SQL.
