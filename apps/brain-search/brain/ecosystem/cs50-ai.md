# CS50 AI

Harvard's free online course on the ideas behind modern AI, taught in Python, with topics such as search, machine learning and large language models. It asks for some Python experience first, and it is also offered on edX with a paid certificate option. Recommended in the Techlahoma Slack as a course for learning AI with Python.

Also called: CS50's Introduction to Artificial Intelligence with Python, CS50 Introduction to AI with Python, CS50AI, Harvard CS50 AI.

**Example:** A technical cofounder works through CS50 AI on weekends to understand how the models in the product actually work.

**Connected on the map to:** AI, LLM, Training, Code.
