# TCC Skills Lab

Tulsa Community College's free training program for adults changing careers or adding tech skills, with virtual programs and employer-designed projects. It builds on the Cyber Skills Center, is supported by the George Kaiser Family Foundation and the Oklahoma Workforce Commission, and has a study space on the second floor of Gradient. Discussed in the Techlahoma Slack as a program that opened enrollment for beginners and career changers in 2026.

Also called: Skills Lab, Tulsa Community College Skills Lab, TCC Skills Lab Workforce Development Hub, Skills Lab Tulsa.

**Example:** A founder submits a small data project to the TCC Skills Lab so learners can work on it and she can meet possible hires.

**Connected on the map to:** Workforce development, Tulsa Community College, Cyber Skills Center, Gradient, George Kaiser Family Foundation, Work-integrated learning, Oklahoma Workforce Commission.
