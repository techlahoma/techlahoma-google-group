# Gemini

Google's AI chat assistant, which people type or talk to. Gemini is also the name of Google's family of AI models, which other products reach through an API. It is the same kind of tool as Claude and ChatGPT.

Also called: Google Gemini, Gemini AI, Gemini app, Google AI, Jemini.

**Example:** A founder asks Gemini to compare three venues for a launch event and list the questions to ask each one.

**Connected on the map to:** AI chat assistant, LLM, Claude, ChatGPT, Google Cloud, Frontier model, Google Developer Group Tulsa, Google.
