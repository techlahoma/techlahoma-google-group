# Techlahoma Slack

Techlahoma's Slack workspace, where technologists across Oklahoma ask questions, share events and post jobs. People join through a sign-up form on Techlahoma's site, and the public channels are part of its free membership. It has channels for introductions, general tech talk, jobs, each user group and each conference, and its code of conduct has Slack rules.

Also called: Techlahoma Slack workspace, Techlahoma chat, Oklahoma tech Slack, techlahoma.slack.com, #introduce-yourself, #tech-talk.

**Example:** A founder joins, says hello in #introduce-yourself and asks a database question in #tech-talk. Two engineers answer the same afternoon.
