# Gradient Technology Incubator

Gradient's incubator for technology companies in Tulsa. Gradient says it is designed for technology-enabled product or service businesses that have already shown some market viability.

Also called: Gradient incubator, Tulsa tech incubator, 36 Degrees North incubator.

**Example:** A software startup with a handful of customers applies to the incubator for workspace and support while it grows.

**Connected on the map to:** Gradient, Incubator, Traction, Coworking space.
