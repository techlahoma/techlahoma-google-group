# Google Cybersecurity Professional Certificate

An online Google Career Certificate in cybersecurity, offered on Coursera. It needs no prior experience and covers Python, Linux and SQL for security work, security monitoring tools, intrusion detection and security frameworks such as those from NIST.

Also called: Google Cybersecurity Certificate, Google cyber cert, Google Cybersecurity Career Certificate.

**Example:** A career changer takes the Google Cybersecurity Certificate before applying for an entry-level security analyst job at a Tulsa company.

**Connected on the map to:** Grow with Google, Coursera, Professional certificate, Workforce development, CompTIA.
