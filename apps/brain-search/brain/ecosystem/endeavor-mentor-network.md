# Endeavor mentorship

Endeavor’s network of business leaders, investors, operators and entrepreneurs who advise selected founders and selection candidates. Mentors share experience and help founders work through business and growth questions. Prospective mentors can contact Endeavor through its official mentor page.

Also called: Endeavor mentor network, Endeavor mentors.

**Example:** A Tulsa founder in Endeavor’s selection process discusses a growth challenge with an experienced operator.

**Connected on the map to:** Endeavor, Mentor, Endeavor Entrepreneur selection, Boddle Learning, Ten-Nine Technologies, Endeavor Heartland ScaleUp.
