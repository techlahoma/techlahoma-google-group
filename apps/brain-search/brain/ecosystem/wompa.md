# WOMPA

A creative compound in a historic Tulsa warehouse with coworking, private offices, studios, galleries and six event venues for small to large events. It offers day memberships and describes itself as an incubator and accelerator for business owners and creative people. Recommended in the Techlahoma Slack as a relaxed venue for a Tulsa hackathon.

Also called: Wompa Tulsa, WOMPA coworking, Wampa, Womba.

**Example:** A founder planning a weekend product sprint rents an event space at WOMPA so the team can work together in one room.

**Connected on the map to:** Coworking space, Event venue, Hackathon, Makerspace, Startup ecosystem.
