# Techlahoma Code of Conduct

The rules for how people treat each other at Techlahoma events, in person and online, and in its Slack. It includes a section of Slack policies and says how to report a problem.

Also called: Techlahoma CoC, Techlahoma Slack policies, Techlahoma rules, Slack policies.

**Example:** Before promoting a product in the Slack, a founder reads the Slack policies to see what counts as spam.
