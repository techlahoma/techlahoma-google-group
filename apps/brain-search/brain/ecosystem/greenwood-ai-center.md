# Greenwood AI Center of Excellence

Black Tech Street's Tulsa initiative for AI capacity, education and innovation. It includes ASPIRE education work and the Greenwood Cyber + AI Lab, developed with Microsoft and SeedAI. Black Tech Street's 2025 announcement of its NVIDIA collaboration names the center as part of that work.

Also called: Microsoft Greenwood AI Center, G-ACE, Greenwood AI/AS Center of Excellence, Greenwood AI Center.
