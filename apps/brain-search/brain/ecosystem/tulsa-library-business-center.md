# Central Library Business Center

A free service of the Tulsa City-County Library at the downtown Central Library. With a library card, founders can use business databases for market, competitor and customer research, and ask staff for help. It also has information on patents and trademarks.

Also called: Central Library, library business center.

**Example:** A founder sizing a market uses the library's business databases to count how many dental offices operate in Oklahoma.

**Connected on the map to:** TAM, Customer discovery, Intellectual property, Target customer, Gradient, Tulsa Tech Week.
