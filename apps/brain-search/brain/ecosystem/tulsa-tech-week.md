# Tulsa Tech Week

A week-long series of tech events across Tulsa, each one hosted by a different person or organization. Techlahoma has been one of its listed partners. It is not a Techlahoma program. Discussed in the Techlahoma Slack as bringing together more than 80 partners hosting free events in its first year, 2025.

Also called: Tech Week, Tulsa Tech Week 2026, tulsatechweek, tech weeks.

**Example:** A founder hosts a demo night during Tulsa Tech Week and attends three other events that week.

**Connected on the map to:** OKC Tech Week, Meetup, Techlahoma, Global Entrepreneurship Week, Startup ecosystem, Build in Tulsa, Gradient, Black Tech Street.
