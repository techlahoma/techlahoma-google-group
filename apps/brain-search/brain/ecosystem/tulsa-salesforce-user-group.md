# Tulsa Salesforce User Group

A Salesforce Trailblazer Community Group in Tulsa that holds talks, release-notes reviews and socials for people who use Salesforce. It has helped bring WeDreaminOK, a Salesforce community conference, to Oklahoma. Discussed in the Techlahoma Slack as a group that hosted a hands-on session on Salesforce's AI agent tools in 2026.

Also called: Salesforce User Group Tulsa, Tulsa Salesforce, Tulsa Trailblazer Community Group, Salesforce Tulsa, Tulsa SFDC user group.

**Example:** A founder choosing a CRM goes to a Tulsa Salesforce User Group meeting to hear how local companies set it up.

**Connected on the map to:** User group, Meetup, AI agent, SaaS, WeDreaminOK, Startup ecosystem.
