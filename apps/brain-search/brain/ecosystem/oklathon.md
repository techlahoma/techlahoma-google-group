# Oklathon

Techlahoma's free open-source hackathon in Oklahoma City, a weekend of pitching ideas, forming teams, building and demoing projects with mentors and workshops. Its 2025 edition had an AI focus and was held at StarSpace46, with Techlahoma among its sponsors. Discussed in the Techlahoma Slack as a hackathon with AI challenges suitable for newcomers, and as having offered an introductory How To Hackathon class in 2024.

Also called: Okla-thon, Oklathon hackathon, Oklahoma hackathon Techlahoma, Ocklathon, Oaklathon.

**Example:** A founder with no team yet pitches an idea on Oklathon's first night and leaves Sunday with a working demo and two people who want to keep building it.

**Connected on the map to:** Hackathon, Techlahoma, Open source, StarSpace46, AI, Mentor, Idea Forge Foundation.
