# Techlahoma Expert AMA

Techlahoma's online question-and-answer series. A host puts questions gathered from the Techlahoma community to a guest expert, then opens the session to live questions. Discussed in the Techlahoma Slack as a monthly virtual series whose topics have included hiring, AI, venture capital, design and legal questions.

Also called: Expert AMAs, Techlahoma AMA, Techlahoma Expert AMAs, Techlahoma AMAs.

**Example:** A founder sends in a question about preparing company data for AI and hears the guest answer it during the lunchtime session.

**Connected on the map to:** AMA, Techlahoma, Google Developer Group Tulsa, Office hours, Mentor.
