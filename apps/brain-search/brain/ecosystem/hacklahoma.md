# Hacklahoma

The student-run hackathon at the University of Oklahoma in Norman.

Also called: Hack-lahoma, Hacklahoma OU, OU hackathon, Hack Oklahoma.

**Example:** A founder looking for a first engineer volunteers as a mentor at Hacklahoma and meets students who build quickly under pressure.

**Connected on the map to:** Hackathon, Talent, HackOKState, Startup Launchpad.
