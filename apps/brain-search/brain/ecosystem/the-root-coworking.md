# The Root Coworking

A coworking company with two locations in Tulsa. It offers flexible workspaces, private offices, meeting rooms, event spaces and podcast studios, and says it welcomes remote workers, startups and community events.

Also called: The Root, Root Coworking, The Root Tulsa, Root East Village, Root Market Station.

**Example:** A three-person startup rents a small office at The Root and books its meeting room for customer calls.

**Connected on the map to:** Coworking space, Gradient, Founder, Meetup, Tulsa Tech Week.
