# Oklahoma CareerTech

The Oklahoma state agency for career and technology education, with tech centers on campuses across the state. Its business and industry services include Apprenticeship Oklahoma, the Oklahoma APEX Accelerator for government contracting and workforce training for employers.

Also called: CareerTech, Career Tech, Oklahoma Department of Career and Technology Education, ODCTE, Oklahoma CareerTech system, career technology centers.

**Example:** A hardware startup asks Oklahoma CareerTech about training for its assembly technicians and about selling to government buyers through the APEX Accelerator.

**Connected on the map to:** Workforce development, Apprenticeship, Government contracting, Talent, Oklahoma Workforce Commission, Pioneer Technology Center Business Incubator.
