# Gemini Notebook

A Google research tool, formerly called NotebookLM, that answers questions using the documents a person adds, with citations, and can turn them into summaries and audio overviews.

Also called: NotebookLM, Notebook LM, Google NotebookLM, notebook lm, Google Gemini Notebook.

**Example:** A founder adds ten grant guidelines to Gemini Notebook and asks which ones fund software startups.

**Connected on the map to:** RAG, Hallucination, Gemini, Google, Startup ecosystem.
