# American Job Centers

A nationwide network of local workforce offices set up under the Workforce Innovation and Opportunity Act and supported by the US Department of Labor. They help job seekers search and train for jobs, and they help employers recruit, match candidates to job requirements, hold job fairs and handle layoffs.

Also called: American Job Center, AJC, AJCs, One-Stop Career Center, one-stop centers, American job centre.

**Example:** A founder opening a warehouse asks the nearest American Job Center to hold a hiring event for the first shift.

**Connected on the map to:** Workforce Innovation and Opportunity Act, US Department of Labor, Workforce development, Talent, Recruiter.
