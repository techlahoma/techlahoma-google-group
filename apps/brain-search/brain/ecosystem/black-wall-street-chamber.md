# Black Wall Street Chamber of Commerce

A membership nonprofit in Tulsa that supports Black-owned businesses in the region. Its Nest Collective is an accelerator for early-stage business owners with coaching, financial literacy, planning and marketing help.

Also called: BWSCC, Black Wall Street Chamber, BWS Chamber, Nest Collective.

**Example:** A new business owner joins the chamber's Nest Collective to get coaching on a first marketing plan.

**Connected on the map to:** Chamber of commerce, Accelerator, Black Tech Street, Nonprofit, Mentor, Greenwood District, Historic Greenwood Chamber of Commerce.
