# Build in Tulsa

A Tulsa organization that gives entrepreneurs accelerators, pre-accelerators, coaching, free coworking, pitch events and a network. It says it serves every stage of company, with a focus on Black founders and other underrepresented founders, honoring the legacy of Black Wall Street. The George Kaiser Family Foundation lists it among the programs it supports, and Build in Tulsa lists Atento Capital among its funding partners. Discussed in the Techlahoma Slack as one of Oklahoma's founder and community resources.

Also called: BIT, Build Tulsa, buildintulsa, Build In Tulsa Inc, Building Tulsa.

**Example:** A founder with an idea and no customers starts in a Build in Tulsa pre-accelerator, then applies to its accelerator a year later once the product has revenue.

**Connected on the map to:** ARCH, W.E. Build, BUILD UP, Blueprint: Tulsa, Product Pitch Night, Build Venture Summit, Accelerator, George Kaiser Family Foundation.
