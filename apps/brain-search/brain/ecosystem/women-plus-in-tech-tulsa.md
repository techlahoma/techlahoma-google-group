# Women+ in Tech of Tulsa

A Tulsa community for women with careers in technology that offers networking, mentorship, professional development and certification and education programs. Its Girls in Tech program introduces students ages 9 to 18 to technology careers. Discussed in the Techlahoma Slack as a group with networking, mentorship, certification support and many free events.

Also called: Women+ in Technology of Tulsa, WIT Tulsa, WIT+ Tulsa, Women in Technology of Tulsa, Women in Tech Tulsa, Women plus in Tech Tulsa.

**Example:** A woman founder joins a Women+ in Tech of Tulsa certification cohort to study for a cloud exam alongside other women in tech.

**Connected on the map to:** Talent, Mentor, Professional certificate, Meetup.
