# ThunderPlains

Techlahoma's annual web and mobile developer conference in Oklahoma City, organized by volunteers. Discussed in the Techlahoma Slack as offering lightning talk opportunities and space for user groups to introduce themselves.

Also called: Thunder Plains, ThunderPlains conference, Thunder Plains conf, thunderplainsconf, ThunderPlains Developer Conference.

**Example:** A Tulsa founder drives to Oklahoma City for ThunderPlains to meet developers from the rest of the state.

**Connected on the map to:** Techlahoma, Tech conference, 200OK, Talent, OKC Tech Week, Apricot, InnoTech Oklahoma, WeDreaminOK.
