# Techlahoma jobs channel

The #jobs channel in the Techlahoma Slack, where members post open tech roles. Techlahoma's Slack policies set the rules for job posts, so read them before posting.

Also called: #jobs, Techlahoma jobs, Techlahoma job board, Oklahoma tech jobs.

**Example:** A founder ready to hire a first engineer posts the role, the location and the pay in #jobs and hears from three candidates.

**Connected on the map to:** Techlahoma Slack, Talent, Recruiter, Software engineer, Equity vs cash compensation.
