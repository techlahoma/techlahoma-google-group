# Tulsa Tech

Tulsa's public career and technology education district. It is a school, not a tech industry group. For business owners it offers small business and nonprofit training, including CO.STARTERS entrepreneur programs, and hosts the Oklahoma APEX Accelerator, which helps companies sell to the government. Discussed in the Techlahoma Slack as offering IT certification training, including CompTIA A+, PC Pro and CCNA. Palo Alto Networks' published Cybersecurity Academy list names it as a member institution of the free school program.

Also called: Tulsa Technology Center, TulsaTech, Tossatech, Tulsa Tech small business, Oklahoma APEX Accelerator, APEX Accelerator.

**Example:** A founder who wants a city contract meets an APEX Accelerator counselor at Tulsa Tech to learn how to register as a government supplier.

**Connected on the map to:** Tulsa Community College, Talent, Mentor, Palo Alto Networks, Tulsa Community Foundation, 1st Step Male Diversion Program, Broken Arrow Public Schools, Tulsa Public Schools.
