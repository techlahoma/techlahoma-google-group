# OSU-Tulsa

Oklahoma State University's downtown Tulsa campus, in the Historic Greenwood District, which offers OSU undergraduate and graduate degrees and short courses for working professionals. Its conference center rents an auditorium, banquet rooms and meeting rooms to businesses and organizations, and it was the venue for the first Tulsa AWS Community Day in 2026.

Also called: Oklahoma State University-Tulsa, Oklahoma State University Tulsa, OSU Tulsa, OSU-Tulsa Conference Center, OSU Tulsa campus, OSUT.
