# Women in Tech Summit

A summit for women in technology and their allies that Oklahoma Women in Technology hosts with InnoTech Oklahoma in Oklahoma City. It has two tracks of sessions, leadership and technology, with local speakers.

Also called: Oklahoma Women in Tech Summit, OKWIT Summit, InnoTech Women's Summit, Women's Summit at InnoTech.

**Example:** A woman founder attends the Women in Tech Summit to hear how other women in Oklahoma built and led technical teams.

**Connected on the map to:** Oklahoma Women in Technology, InnoTech Oklahoma, Tech conference, Talent, Mentor.
