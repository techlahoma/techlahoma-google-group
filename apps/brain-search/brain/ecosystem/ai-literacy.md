# AI literacy

Enough understanding of AI to use it well and safely: what it is good and bad at, how to write a prompt, how to check its work and what data not to share with it.

Also called: AI fluency, AI skills, AI training for staff, AI basics.

**Example:** An AI literacy workshop teaches office staff to spot made-up answers and to keep client data out of public chat tools.

**Connected on the map to:** AI, Prompt, Hallucination, AI use policy, Data literacy, Change management, Continuing professional education, Bloom's Taxonomy.
