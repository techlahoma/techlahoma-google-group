# Gemini (app)

The Gemini app is Google's consumer AI assistant on web and mobile.

- Google's overview page describes Gemini as "an interface to a multimodal LLM (handling text, audio, images and more)," with "mobile and web experiences."
- Uses named on the page: summarizing long documents, help with coding, drafting outlines, generating images and explaining complex concepts.
- The page mentions Gems, which let users customize Gemini with specific instructions. (checked 2026-11-18)
- For developers, the same models are available through the [Gemini API](gemini-api.md) and [Google AI Studio](google-ai-studio.md).
