# Gemma

Gemma is Google DeepMind's family of open models.

- Google DeepMind calls them "Our most capable open models," built to run "from cloud servers to laptops and even phones."
- Latest generation as of 2026-11-18: Gemma 4 (introduced April 2026), in E2B and E4B sizes for mobile and IoT, and 12B, 26B and 31B sizes for personal computers.
- Related releases listed: Gemma 4 QAT, DiffusionGemma, EmbeddingGemma 2 and MedGemma 1.5 4B.
- Available through Kaggle, Hugging Face, Ollama, Keras, PyTorch, Gemma.cpp, JAX, Google AI Edge, Google Cloud, Android AI Core, LM Studio and Unsloth. Gemma 4 31B can be tried in [Google AI Studio](google-ai-studio.md).
- The ai.google.dev/gemma URL now redirects to the DeepMind page.
