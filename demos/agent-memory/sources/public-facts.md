# Clippings: tonight's event and the groups behind it

Real, public facts, grouped by the page they came from. Checked 2026-11-18.

## From the event page

Source: https://luma.com/b7eptsqo

- "Google Dev × Women+ in Tech: Actual Productivity with AI" is on Thursday, November 19, 2026, 6–8 PM CST, at Gitwit, 301 E Archer St, Tulsa.
- GDG Tulsa and Women+ in Technology of Tulsa teamed up with Techlahoma for an evening about getting real work off your plate with AI.
- No engineering background is needed, attending requires no install or purchase, and registration is free with host approval.
- Hosts listed on the event page: Techlahoma Foundation, Sam Carlton, Zain Khan, Tran Trinh, Jess Trippiedi and Valerie Feral.

## From the GDG Tulsa chapter page

Source: https://gdg.community.dev/gdg-tulsa/

- GDG Tulsa is a newly launched Google Developer Group for the Tulsa developer community.
- The chapter page says it is independent and not affiliated with or endorsed by Google.
- It is open to all backgrounds and experience levels, and the page listed 58 members.
- The organizer listed is Zain Khan, GDG Organizer.

## From the Techlahoma site

Source: https://www.techlahoma.org/

- The site calls Techlahoma "Oklahoma's Largest Technology Network" with over 7,000 members.
- The site says "The Techlahoma Foundation is a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization."
- Its mission: "We enrich Oklahoma's technologists of all backgrounds through education, connection, and opportunity."
- The site reports over 18 Meetup groups established and over 2,359 events hosted.

## From the Gitwit site

Source: https://gitwit.com/

- Gitwit describes itself as "A venture studio driven by deep human research," located in Tulsa, Oklahoma, on East Archer Street.
- It builds products with researchers, strategists, designers and engineers; full-time co-founders then take over the companies it launches.

## From the Tulsa Ecosystem Map

- Women+ in Tech of Tulsa is a Tulsa community for women with careers in technology that offers networking, mentorship, professional development, and certification and education programs. Source: https://ecosystem.samcarlton.com/women-plus-in-tech-tulsa
- Its Girls in Tech program introduces students ages 9 to 18 to technology careers. Source: https://ecosystem.samcarlton.com/women-plus-in-tech-tulsa
- It is also called Women+ in Technology of Tulsa and WIT Tulsa. Source: https://ecosystem.samcarlton.com/women-plus-in-tech-tulsa
- GDG Tulsa launched in 2026. It is a partner of Techlahoma, not one of its user groups. Source: https://ecosystem.samcarlton.com/gdg-tulsa
- Techlahoma launched in 2014 and is the official tech partner of GDG Tulsa. Source: https://ecosystem.samcarlton.com/techlahoma
- Gitwit is the local sponsor of GDG Tulsa, and 200OK 2026 was held there. Source: https://ecosystem.samcarlton.com/gitwit
