# Techlahoma

Techlahoma is an Oklahoma technology network and non-profit.

- The site calls Techlahoma "Oklahoma's Largest Technology Network" with over 7,000 members. ([source](https://www.techlahoma.org/))
- The site says "The Techlahoma Foundation is a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization." ([source](https://www.techlahoma.org/))
- Its mission: "We enrich Oklahoma's technologists of all backgrounds through education, connection, and opportunity." ([source](https://www.techlahoma.org/))
- The site reports over 18 Meetup groups established and over 2,359 events hosted. ([source](https://www.techlahoma.org/))
- Techlahoma launched in 2014 and is the official tech partner of GDG Tulsa. ([source](https://ecosystem.samcarlton.com/techlahoma))
