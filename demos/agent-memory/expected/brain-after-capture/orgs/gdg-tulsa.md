# GDG Tulsa

GDG Tulsa is a newly launched Google Developer Group for the Tulsa developer community.

- The chapter page says it is independent and not affiliated with or endorsed by Google. ([source](https://gdg.community.dev/gdg-tulsa/))
- It is open to all backgrounds and experience levels, and the page listed 58 members. ([source](https://gdg.community.dev/gdg-tulsa/))
- The organizer listed is Zain Khan, GDG Organizer. ([source](https://gdg.community.dev/gdg-tulsa/))
- GDG Tulsa launched in 2026. It is a partner of Techlahoma, not one of its user groups. ([source](https://ecosystem.samcarlton.com/gdg-tulsa))
