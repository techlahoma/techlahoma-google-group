# Google Dev × Women+ in Tech: Actual Productivity with AI

An evening in Tulsa about getting real work off your plate with AI.

- It is on Thursday, November 19, 2026, 6–8 PM CST, at Gitwit, 301 E Archer St, Tulsa. ([source](https://luma.com/b7eptsqo))
- GDG Tulsa and Women+ in Technology of Tulsa teamed up with Techlahoma for an evening about getting real work off your plate with AI. ([source](https://luma.com/b7eptsqo))
- No engineering background is needed, attending requires no install or purchase, and registration is free with host approval. ([source](https://luma.com/b7eptsqo))
- Hosts listed on the event page: Techlahoma Foundation, Sam Carlton, Zain Khan, Tran Trinh, Jess Trippiedi and Valerie Feral. ([source](https://luma.com/b7eptsqo))
