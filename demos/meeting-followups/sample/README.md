# Sample meetings

**Fictional sample data for the workshop.** Every person, company, nonprofit, and
email address here is invented. Nothing in this folder describes a real meeting.

The three meetings share one attendee, **Dana Whitfield**, an account manager at
a small print shop (Quillhaven Print Co.) who also volunteers on a food pantry
committee. Her week is the "my action items this week" example.

| File | Meeting | Date | Shape |
|---|---|---|---|
| `01-pantry-dinner-transcript.md` | Maple Hollow Community Pantry, Harvest Dinner planning committee | Monday, November 16, 2026 | Raw speaker-labeled transcript |
| `02-fernbrook-call-notes.md` | Sales call with Fernbrook Dental Group | Tuesday, November 17, 2026 | Messy bullet notes Dana typed during the call |
| `03-portal-status-notes.md` | Reorder portal launch, weekly status meeting | Wednesday, November 18, 2026 | Meeting minutes |
| `followup-message.md` | Correction to meeting 3 | Thursday, November 19, 2026 | A chat message sent the next morning |
| `people.md` | Who is who, with made-up email addresses | | Roster |

`followup-message.md` was sent **after** meeting 3 and changes some of what was
decided there. The later message wins.

"This week" in this sample means Monday, November 16 through Sunday, November 22, 2026.
